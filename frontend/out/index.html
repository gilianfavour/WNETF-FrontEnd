Who We Are

Building Opportunities for Students Across West Nile

The West Nile Education Trust Fund (WNETF) provides scholarships, financial support, and educational development initiatives to empower students and schools across the West Nile Region.

Our Mission

To provide scholarships, grants, and financial support to students and educational institutions to improve learning outcomes across West Nile.

Our Vision

Empowered and well-educated communities in the West Nile Region through accessible quality education.

Our Impact in Numbers

27 Students Supported

Beneficiaries of scholarships and grants (as of now).

7 Public Universities

Supported students studying at Gulu, Makerere, MUST, Muni, KYU, Busitema, and Kabale.

Areas of Strategic Focus

Mindset change, leadership development, data and information, strategic communication, policy advocacy, partnerships, sponsorship & support.

Ongoing Programs

Continuous support for sustainable education growth in West Nile.
